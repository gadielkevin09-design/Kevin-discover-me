RA 9729

Climate Change Act of 2009

Section 2 • Declaration of Policy

📖 The Provision

Section 2 – Declaration of Policy

The government recognizes the importance of addressing climate change and protecting the environment for both present and future generations. It promotes sustainable development and the protection of people and the environment from the effects of climate change.

💭 My Reflection

Section 2 of RA 9729 made me realize that protecting the environment is not only the responsibility of the government. It also affects our lives and the lives of future generations. Climate change can cause problems such as stronger storms, floods, rising temperatures, and damage to nature.

For me, this provision is important because we all depend on the environment every day. We need clean air, clean water, food, and a safe place to live. Sometimes, people may not notice the effects of climate change immediately, but small changes in our environment can become bigger problems over time.

I also realized that ordinary people can contribute to protecting the environment. Even simple actions like reducing waste, saving electricity and water, avoiding unnecessary plastic, and keeping our surroundings clean can make a difference. These actions may seem small, but if many people do them consistently, they can have a bigger impact.

This provision also reminds me that we should think about the future, not only about what is convenient for us today. I believe that protecting the environment is a shared responsibility. If we become more aware of climate change and do our part, we can help create a safer and healthier environment for ourselves and for the generations that will come after us.

🌿

Small Actions, Bigger Impact

Taking care of our environment today can help protect the future of the people who come after us.
